Sandbox SDK creates a sandbox, moves files, and runs commands through one TypeScript client. You still choose the adapter, own that account, and supply credentials.

This page covers where to go next.

**[Compare adapters](/docs/get-started/compare)**: what is portable and what throws `unsupported`.

**[Install](/docs/get-started/install)**: package, runtime floor, and peers.

**[First sandbox](/docs/get-started/first-sandbox)**: `e2b().create()` or `memory().create()`.

**[Operations](/docs/api/sandboxes)**: files, commands, git, processes, ports.

**[Providers](/docs/providers)**: choose the runtime, persistence model, and optional capabilities.

**[Agents](/docs/integrations)**: install the agent skill or connect AI SDK, HarnessAgent, Eve, and Mastra.

**[Migrate from 0.2](/docs/guides/migrate-0-2)**: what stays, what throws, and the new factory.

Do not assume ports, snapshots, or live tests. Railway is experimental.
